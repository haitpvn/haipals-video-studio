# /brag plan — Haipals Đi Đâu (HaiPals Travel and Visa)

**What it is:** A UK-based Vietnamese travel & visa agency: visa, documents, flights and hotels, with a personal advisor who answers everything.
**Who for:** Vietnamese people in the UK who hold a passport but don't know where to start.
**Sets it apart:** "Có một bạn riêng đồng hành" — a personal companion, honest advice, no hidden fees. Mascot: the aviator black cat.
**Hook:** The mascot cat, teary at the front door, + the page's own opening line: "Cầm hộ chiếu mà chưa biết bắt đầu từ đâu?"
**Tone:** friendly and trustworthy, for Vietnamese clients in the UK (warm, clean, playful — app-store-ish feature beats).
**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30fps, 23.5s, 120 BPM (cuts on the beat).
**Identity:** cream #FFF6EC, brand red #E5402B, ink #141414, sky #BFE3F7; Pacifico (script wordmark, as on the cover), Be Vietnam Pro (UI/body). Logo from Drive (Logo for HAIPALS Travel.svg), mascot from Drive (meo-toan-than.png).
